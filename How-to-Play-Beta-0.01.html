LBSgame Stock Sim Trader

A classroom virtual-stock-trading game with real-time US quotes and virtual money.

Beta 0.01 · How to Play

1 What is this?

This is a single HTML file you open in any browser. You and your classmates each get a virtual wallet, buy real US stocks with real-time prices, and sell whenever you want. The goal: end the week with the biggest total assets.

Real quotes, virtual money. Nothing here is real investing and nothing is saved to the cloud — it only lives on your own browser.

2 Getting started (30 seconds)

  1. Double-click LBSgame.Stock.Sim.Trader.Beta.0.01.html to open it in Chrome / Edge / Safari / mobile browser.
  2. Wait 2–3 seconds for the prices to load (top right shows "Updated ...").
  3. If it says "Couldn't load live quotes", check your internet and press Refresh quotes.

3 Add players

Scroll to "Add player", type a nickname (e.g. Alex), enter a starting capital (default $1000), then press + Add player.

  • Each player can have a different starting capital — that's fine, it's just for fun.
  • Switch between players any time with the dropdown in the dark top bar.
  • The dark bar shows the current player's Cash / Positions value / Total assets.
  • Press × next to a player to remove them (you'll be asked to confirm).

4 Buy a stock

  1. Make sure the player you want is selected in the dark top bar.
  2. In any quote card (Roblox, Nvidia, etc.), type the dollar amount you want to spend in the input.
  3. Press Buy. Cash is deducted instantly and the stock shows up in "Current player's holdings".
Tip: enter all your remaining cash for an "all-in" move.
If you see "Insufficient cash", enter a smaller number — the game won't let you spend more than you have.

5 Sell (cash out)

Scroll to "Current player's holdings". On each row press Sell, then:

  • Leave the quantity field blank to sell everything of that stock.
  • Or type a number of shares to sell only part.
  • Press Confirm sell. Cash goes back into the player's wallet at the live price.

The game automatically tracks:

  • Unrealized P&L — profit/loss on what you still hold.
  • Realized P&L — profit/loss on what you've already sold.
  • Total P&L = unrealized + realized.

7 Save & Reset

  • Save — writes everything to your browser's local storage. Next time you open the file on the same browser, your players and holdings come back.
  • Reset — wipes everything and starts a brand-new game (you'll be asked to confirm).
Prices refresh automatically every 60 seconds during US market hours. Outside trading hours you'll see the previous close.

8 Suggested class rules

  • Everyone starts with the same capital (e.g. $1000) and trades for one week.
  • Each player picks their own nickname; no peeking at others' screens until Friday.
  • Winner = highest Total assets at the end. Tie = whoever saved the most cash.
  • Optional twist: every day the teacher picks one "industry news" (e.g. AI up / retail down) and players react.
